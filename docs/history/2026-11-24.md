## 1. The Pi Zero W, measured on the current build

Dan asked whether freedj still runs on the original Zero W (ARMv6, one
core). It had lost its Wi-Fi config (empty netplan files after an update);
a NetworkManager keyfile on the card brought it back.

On KMSDRM, fullscreen, WSOLA, the current build matches October's at the
same layout: **50 fps in the XDJ-1000MK2 layout (20 ms a frame), 21 fps in
the CDJ-3000 one (47 ms)**. Under the desktop (labwc), 18 and 11. The
difference is the layout, not the code: the CDJ-3000 screen is 1.7× the
pixels with a full-width waveform, and on one core the per-phase timings
show where the frame waits (docs/BENCHMARKS.md, `c5754eb`). Offered, not
done: make the XDJ layout the default on the ARMv6 build.

## 2. README: the feature list, and what the CDJ-3000 images are

The README's short list became a full feature list by area, matching
docs/FEATURES.md (`6e0e055`).

Dan asked to say that releases ship a UPD and an ISO for real CDJ-3000
hardware. They don't: `CDJ3Kv<NNN>-OPENDECK-EMU.iso`/`.UPD` are for
cdj3k-emu only, and docs/hardware/cdj-3000-upd.md says the emulator UPD
must never go near a deck (it carries no drivers for the real board). The
deck UPD is designed, not built. The README was not changed to say
otherwise.

## 3. The mini jog ring with LM393 boards

After printing the carrier (`hardware/cad/jogring/mini_lm393.py`):

- The boards sat too high: they now sit **1.5 mm into the carrier's
  deck**, top flush with it. The beam is then under the ring's fin, so a
  **3.8 mm spacer** under each board brings it back to the slots' middle
  (`SPACER`; depends on the uncalipered beam height) (`abaefc3`).
- The pocket walls only stand 0.8 mm over the deck; the bearing posts
  rise straight to the axle and round over it at the stub's offset; every
  M3 hole in the carrier has a 90° chamfer to Ø6.4 underneath (`f43fd0c`).

`make jogring`: no clashes, held every way, 256-count quadrature (with the
spacer).

## 4. Mirroring freedj-esp's firmware decks (FOLLOW without a dbserver)

Dan asked whether Overdeck's `follow = N` could mirror freedj-esp's
CDJ-2000 firmware. It couldn't: the firmware's status carried a fixed
track ID from its capture template, and it served neither dbserver nor NFS.

**Firmware** (freedj-esp `aae9858`):
- the status names the loaded track by its rekordbox ID, read from the
  stick's `export.pdb` (`main/pdb.cpp`: the track table, ID to analysis
  path);
- `main/nfsd.cpp` serves the stick read-only as a player does: portmap on
  111, mount v1 and NFSv2 on 2049, export `/C/`, UTF-16LE names, READs up
  to 32 KB;
- the stack sends big datagrams as IP fragments and resets every TCP
  connection, so a peer asking for a dbserver learns at once there is none.

**freedj** (`624209d`): a LINK load from a player with no dbserver finds
the path in the stick's `export.pdb` over NFS by the status's rekordbox ID.
The NFS probe is 300 ms a try, so a player serving nothing still fails fast
(an existing test caught the 12 s version).

**Tested:** `tools/test_nfs.py` (15 checks from a peer on the emulator's
segment) and `tools/test_nfs_freedj.py` (freedj's own NFS client through a
UDP relay). End to end on djlink0 with a tap for the emulator
(`tools/emu_djlink.py`, `abfea28`): freedj `--follow 2` loaded the track
(10.6 MB in 2.4 s with the stick's grid), held its playhead within ±1.2 ms
of the deck's beat packets, paused within 1.2 ms of the deck's position and
resumed with PLAY.

A stock CDJ-2000 is mirrored too, but only partly: it serves its stick and
names its track, but sends no beat number, so there is no playhead.

## 5. freedj-esp on a second board: the XDJ-1000MK2

Dan asked how close the XDJ-1000MK2 (his unit) is to the CDJ-2000. Both
are SH-4A (SH7724 against SH7763), with the same kind of Ethernet and USB,
so the deck code carries over and the board layer is what changes.

- **The board split** (`11f22c7`): `main/board.h` holds the differences
  (DRAM at 0x08000000, a 41.67 MHz peripheral clock, the Ethernet at
  0xA4600000 with its link bit the other way round, the stick on the second
  USB block, a 32-byte panel frame). `make BOARD=xdj1000mk2` builds into
  `build-xdj1000mk2/`; the tests take `BOARD=`.
- **The screen** (`f4f10fd`): the CDJ-2000's drawing moved from
  `gui/gui.c` to `common/deck_draw.c` with its layout as data. The
  CDJ-2000 keeps every number; the XDJ's main board draws the same design
  at 800×480 RGB565 into two framebuffers the SH7724's LCDC scans out, with
  fonts baked 1.7× (`docs/screen_xdj1000mk2.png`).
- **Not ported:** the C6747's audio (a silent 44.1 kHz sink stands in, so
  timing and Link behave), and the LCDC's real-panel setup (clock, pins,
  timings).
- **Guesses (⚠):** the emulator gives the XDJ's tempo slider no centre
  reference, so it is read against mid travel; the DIRECTION lever is held
  at FWD by the emulator, so REV can't be tested.

`make test BOARD=xdj1000mk2`: 7 of 7. The CDJ-2000's 9 still pass (its
screen test's "paused, stands still" failed once in a full run and passed
three times alone: a timing flake under load).

Nothing has run on a real XDJ. Dan's unit is the Link reference, and there
is no tested way back to stock firmware yet.

## 6. Overdeck: what it is, and stems for every deck

Written up in freedj-overdeck `docs/BOOTH-HOST.md`:
- Overdeck framed as **the booth's host**, not DJ mixer software; the
  proving use case is a virtual deck beside real CDJs.
- Stems for every deck: serve them to decks that take them; a premixed
  file for stock decks (fixed versions only, since a player reads ahead);
  the **shadow deck** for live stems on any deck (FOLLOW plus Overdeck
  playing the stems under the mirrored playhead).
- Spotify: back in DJ software since September 2025 as a licensed
  integration, without stems; not a way in.

Overdeck run with deck 2 following the emulated XDJ: it loaded the track
from the XDJ's export.pdb over NFS in 2.5 s.
