BROWSE A CDJ-3000 OVER PRO DJ LINK: every category the player lists (ARTIST, ALBUM, GENRE, KEY, BPM, PLAYLIST, HISTORY, FOLDER …) opens, in the unit's own track table with blue or 3-band waveform previews, cover art and an INFO pane. Tracks loaded from it come with their title, artist, key, hot cues in their colours, memory cues, loops and phrases. Browsing a player never freezes the deck, even if it leaves the network.

PHRASES on the CDJ-3000 screen: the track's intro, verses, choruses and outro in rekordbox's colours under the overall waveform (MENU → WAVEFORM DIVISIONS).

OUTPUT GAIN in MENU: turn the deck's output down when the iPad or your interface is too hot at the volume you want to leave it on. It never boosts and never clicks.

LIGHT MODE in MENU: the screen on a white background, for bright rooms.

ABOUT, the last row of MENU: the version and the deck's network addresses.

STEMS: while a track splits, the screen shows which parts have their stems yet.

MORE LIKE THE UNIT: rekordbox hot loops play as loops; IN sets the cue point too, as IN/CUE; TAG TRACK works from the playback screen; long titles scroll through their field; the source tile shows the stick's colour and player number; PLAY LIST opens the playlists of the source you're browsing.
