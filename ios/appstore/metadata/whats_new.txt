Plays nicely with CDJ-3000s and rekordbox, and analyses your tracks more like rekordbox does.

PRO DJ LINK: a CDJ-3000 on the same network now lists OpenDeck's music in SOURCE, browses it by artist, album, track, key, playlist, history and matching, and loads and plays it with OpenDeck's beat grid, cues and colour waveforms. rekordbox shows OpenDeck as a player. PLAYER No. AUTO picks a free player number by itself, like a CDJ.

ANALYSIS: tracks without a key tag get a detected key, named as rekordbox names it. Beat grids land on the beat and BPMs match rekordbox's closely. OpenDeck also marks phrases (intro, verse, chorus, outro), which a CDJ-3000 can show as coloured bands on the waveform.

FIXES: Apple Lossless (ALAC) and mono tracks load. MP3s were 24 ms off from rekordbox and the players; they now line up. Big folders on a linked player are no longer cut short.
