STEMS: split any track into drums, vocals and the rest, right on your iPad. Turn on MENU > STEMS and the app fetches its separation model by itself the first time (about 290 MB, once). Stems play while the track is still being split, and the model no longer stays in memory after a split. Stems prepared on a USB stick with OverCue now play within a fraction of a second of loading.

KEY SHIFT: move the track up or down a semitone at a time without touching the tempo, with RESET to go back. The KEY readouts show the new key.

HOT CUE COLOURS: hot cues from a rekordbox stick show in the colours you gave them in rekordbox, on the pads and the waveforms, and other players that load the track from OpenDeck see them too.

WAVEFORM COLOR: BLUE, RGB or 3BAND in MENU, with 3BAND by default.

PRO DJ LINK: CDJ-3000s and lighting or timecode tools get the deck's exact play position many times a second, its loops, its hot and memory cues, and each track's key in their browse lists.

FIXES: rekordbox tracks with cue points kept their beat grid and cues (they could lose both), and hot cues land on the right pads. SLIP works with hot cues and scratching. Loop keys act the moment they are pressed. TEMPO RANGE no longer moves the tempo. The phase meter no longer jumps a beat. Touch keys no longer miss quick taps. 32-bit FLAC plays, and AIFF titles are clean.
