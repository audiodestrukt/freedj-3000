DVS: play your tracks from a turntable with Serato Control Vinyl (or a Control CD) through a USB audio interface. MENU → DVS: ABS follows the needle (drop it anywhere), REL follows speed and direction only, INT hands the deck back. Forward, backward, back-cueing and scratching, with the record setting the tempo on LINK. OpenDeck asks for microphone access to read the record; no audio leaves the device.

RGB WAVEFORMS AS ON A CDJ-3000: the RGB colour mode was far too red. It now colours the main and overall waveforms the way a CDJ-3000 does, using rekordbox's own RGB waveform when a track has one.
