UI fixes and a cooler device.

iPhone: CUE, PLAY and the MENU rows now respond exactly where they are drawn, and switching pages no longer stops the deck.

Two OpenDecks on one network can browse and load from each other: an iPhone and an iPad, or two iPads.

Much less heat over a set: a paused deck renders at a low rate, and its audio and Link threads sleep instead of polling. INFO shows the app's CPU use.
