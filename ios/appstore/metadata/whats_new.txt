STEMS FROM ANOTHER DECK: load a track from another OpenDeck and open STEMS, and that deck splits it for you, starting where you are playing, so the stems are ready in seconds instead of waiting for a full split. Finished splits are kept for the next load.

STEMS FOR THE NETWORK: with a separation model downloaded, OpenDeck can split tracks for other players and stemd clients on the network, using this device's own model.

FIXED: tracks reached by FOLDER on a rekordbox USB drive now show their preview waveform, BPM and key.
