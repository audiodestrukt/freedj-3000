Cues for other players, a three-colour waveform, and wider file support.

WAVEFORM COLOR: MENU → WAVEFORM COLOR picks BLUE, RGB or 3BAND, and remembers your choice. 3BAND is the new default: lows blue, mids amber, highs white.

STEMS: on tracks with prepared stems (MENU → STEMS on), the enlarged waveform now shows only the parts you are playing: mute the drums and the kicks leave the picture, and FULL MIX brings the whole track back. In the second player layout the STEMS keys show each part's level, and BROWSE marks tracks that have stems with a small "S".

PRO DJ LINK: players that load a track from OpenDeck now get its hot cues and memory cues. Lighting, video and timecode tools can follow OpenDeck's play position and active loop, and tools that look up track titles directly now find them. A pitched OpenDeck now reports the right tempo, so decks synced to it and lighting get the correct BPM.

FIXES: touch keys respond reliably, including with another finger on the jog or the tempo fader. SLIP now works with held hot cues and scratching. LOOP IN and LOOP OUT set the loop where you press. TEMPO RANGE no longer moves the tempo fader. The fader stays where it is and the tempo follows the new range, so +3% on ±6 becomes +8% on ±16. 32-bit FLAC files load and play. AIFF files show their key, BPM and artwork, and no longer end in a short burst of noise. OGG files with tags open. Files at 88.2 kHz and above get a beat grid that lands on the beat (they are analysed again once). In the second player layout, the 16 and 32 marks under the waveform stay still.
