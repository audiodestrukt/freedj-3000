Now on iPhone. Two landscape pages: the screen, and the controls (jog, tempo fader, transport, loops) with the beat meter and BPM in view. Flip with the button or a two-finger swipe.

Share your music over Pro DJ Link: the app now shows up in other players' LINK menus as a media source, with tags, BPM, beat grid and waveforms.

Loading no longer interrupts playback: tracks decode in the background with a LOADING readout, and loads from linked players are many times faster.

The LINK list names each player's media as the players do, and opens their category menu.
