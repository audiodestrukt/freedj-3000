Fixes and a cooler phone.

iPhone: touch targets now land where they are drawn (CUE, PLAY and the MENU rows were off by the safe-area insets), and flipping pages no longer stops the deck.

Two OpenDecks on one network can browse and load from each other: an iPhone and an iPad, or two iPads.

Much less heat over a set: a paused deck renders at a low rate and its audio and Link threads sleep instead of polling. INFO now shows the app's own CPU use, and Documents/opendeck-perf.log records it for the Files app.
