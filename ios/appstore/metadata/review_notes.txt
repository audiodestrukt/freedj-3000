OpenDeck DJ is a standalone single-deck DJ player; no external hardware or account is needed to review it. A demo track is pre-loaded — tap PLAY/PAUSE, use the jog wheel, tempo fader, CUE, LOOP IN/OUT and the on-screen BROWSE / PERFORM keys.

To load your own music: copy audio files into Files → On My iPhone / On My iPad → OpenDeck DJ, then tap BROWSE (or the FILE key), turn the BROWSE knob and press it to LOAD.

On iPhone (new in 0.2) the app is landscape-only and has two pages: SCREEN (the player display with BROWSE, CUE and PLAY beside it) and CONTROLS (jog wheel, tempo fader, loops). Flip with the page button in the bottom margin or a two-finger swipe up/down.

On first launch iOS asks for Local Network permission. This is used only for Pro DJ Link on the local network: beat-syncing with Pioneer CDJ/XDJ players if any are present, and letting those players browse and load the app's music folder. Denying it leaves every other feature working. Nothing is sent off the device.
